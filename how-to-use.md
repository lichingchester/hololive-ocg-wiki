# [WIP] Welcome to Hololive OCG Wiki

This comprehensive guide will help you navigate and make the most of the Hololive Official Card Game wiki. Whether you're a new player or an experienced duelist, you'll find everything you need to search for cards, build decks, and share your creations with the community.

## Searching/Filtering for Cards

### Basic Search

Use the search bar at the top to find cards by name, ability text, or any other card information.
![Search Cards Screenshot](public/how-to-use/searching-cards.png)

```
Examples: "Sora", "Collab", "Blue", "Debut"
```

- You can search in multiple languages (cross languages supported) - the wiki supports Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese, Indonesian, Korean, and Thai
- Search results update in real-time as you type
- No typo search supported.
